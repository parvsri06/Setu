Disaster Survey — Mobile UI Proposal

An offline-first Android app for collecting flood-damage and casualty surveys where there is no internet. Field data is saved locally, moved between nearby phones, and uploaded when a connection appears. Interactive prototype — survey fields follow the attached "Setu Disaster Survey" PDF.
